Rules

Standard CTF etiquette applies. These are the rules we'll enforce.

  1. No flag sharing between teams.

    Scores should show what your team solved.

  2. No attacking the CTF infrastructure.

    The challenges are the targets. The platform is not.

  3. AI-Assisted Problem Solving Policy.

    If you use AI to wholesale the challenge, you just suck. You are allowed to use AI for research, learning, debugging, and programming assistance with unfamiliar programming languages, libraries, and tools. However, using AI to directly solve a challenge, obtain a flag, or generate a complete challenge solution will result in disqualification.

  4. No brute-forcing the flag submission endpoint.

    It slows CTFd down for everyone.

  5. Follow standard CTF etiquette.

    Be a good competitor and treat other teams and organisers with respect.

Unsure about something? Ask an organiser on our Discord before you try it.
